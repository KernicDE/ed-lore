A quadruple-focus initiative has been launched aiming to test the improved capabilities of the recently announced Anaconda Mk II.
To help celebrate the release of the new ship, Faulcon DeLacy has partnered with several organisations to track various ship performance data for comparison with the Anaconda Mk II. Independent pilots are invited to put the new vessel through its paces across mining, trading, combat and exploration.
Frank Endis, Chief of Design commented in a statement issued to Vox Galactica that “most new ship launches focus on testing one or two new capabilities, but the Anaconda has always proven itself as an all-round vessel.”
“It makes sense to put it through its paces against everything that it can do.”
Pilots are invited to support the Anaconda Mk II’s launch by registering at HD 232819, AD Leonis, Reorte and HR 8061 before taking up one or more initiatives. While any vessel can be used to provide comparative data, the Anaconda Mk II’'s performance is of particular interest to analysts.
