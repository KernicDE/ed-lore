Faulcon DeLacy has announced the first major revision of the Anaconda ship model since its introduction in 2856.
Expanding on its predecessor’s multi-purpose capabilities, the Anaconda Mk II prioritises exceptional versatility and was described as “one of our finest large ships” by Chief of Design Frank Endis:
“We took the opportunity to hone the original Anaconda’s advantages as a flexible, general-purpose option. Pilots will be able to equip their Mk II as a credible trading, mining, or combat choice whenever needed at any port with outfitting bays.”
“It still excels as a large exploration vessel as well, of course! Our design focus was on making sure all potential roles were treated with equal attention.”
The Anaconda Mk II comes with an SCO-optimised FSD, a Size 6 optional internal slot, and Mk II armour slots and optimised hardpoint that can mount the new ‘mining volley repeater’ - a multi-barrel excavation tool used for laser mining which uses random sequencing to produce abrasion chunks.
The Anaconda Mk II is immediately available as an early-release purchase and will later transition to wider shipyard availability.
