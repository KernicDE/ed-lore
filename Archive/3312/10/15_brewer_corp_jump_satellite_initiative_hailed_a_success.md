Independent pilots rallied to provide Brewer Corp with the necessary commodities to release its new 'jump satellites' on schedule.
The hardware - a highly modified version of the nav beacon - emits a signal which fleet carrier computers can target, avoiding bottlenecks in systems where no planetary bodies are present.
"We're extremely grateful for the support and the number of supplied commodities received," said Madelyn Teague, CEO of Brewer Corporation.
"Our factories are now working hard to put the new satellites into service with the correct signal calibration settings."
Transmitting to Vox Galactica from the outpost at Rackham's Peak in HIP 58832, Zachary Rackham welcomed the initiative and thanked those taking part, stating that "it was about time that Brewer solved this problem that prevented more visitors arriving at the very top of the galaxy!"
Brewer confirmed that the new jump satellites will begin deployment to the HD 105341 system in the near future, expanding carrier capacity close to Rackham’s Peak.
Contributing pilots can now collect their rewards at the megaship Zero Squared in the HIP 74116 system.
