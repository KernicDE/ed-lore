Brewer Corporation is manufacturing what it calls 'jump satellites' to enable more fleet carriers to target a system with no planetary bodies.
Under existing circumstances, only one fleet carrier can remain stationary in a system of this type, preventing other carriers from targeting and continuing their journey.
"It's especially problematic for explorers in the furthest reaches of the galaxy where star density is at its lowest," remarked Brewer Corporation CEO Madelyn Teague in an interview with Vox Galactica.
"Brewer engineers successfully tested a significant modification to existing beacon amplification. Our prototypes were successful in mimicking the planetary body signal used by fleet carrier jump computers, helping carrier crews safely traverse to a location without a planet or moon present."
Teague's statement concluded with a call to action for independent pilots to deliver certain commodities to ensure the first batch of jump satellites is ready on time.
"With enough of the necessary components, we can deploy jump satellites to fleet carrier bottlenecks around Rackham’s Peak."
Interested pilots should head to the megaship Zero Squared in the HIP 74116 system.
