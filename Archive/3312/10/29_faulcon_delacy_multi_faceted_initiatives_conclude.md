Faulcon DeLacy confirmed in a press statement today that the quadruple trials of the new Anaconda Mk II had been 'incredibly valuable’.
Data received across all four initiatives showed that independent pilots had put the new vessel through its paces across a number of activities.
Speaking with Vox Galactica, Frank Endis, Chief of Design stated that “The Mk II is an extremely capable all-round ship, and we’ve worked hard to improve on the original design.”
“Its flexibility, coupled with the new armour upgrades and internal slot options, should make it a very attractive ship for pilots looking for a Jack of all trades.”
With the trials now concluded, Faulcon DeLacy hopes that the Anaconda Mk II will quickly be adopted by those looking for a larger, general-purpose ship.
The Anaconda Mk II is currently available for exclusive purchase initially and will transition to limited shipyard inventories at a later date.
