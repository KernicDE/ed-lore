The Independent Detention Foundation has confirmed that three maximum security prisoners evaded recapture following the breakout in Redonesses.
Despite system authority attempts to elicit information from the Clan of Redonesses faction, the whereabouts of the three fugitives remains unclear.
A spokesperson for The Independent Detention Foundation confirmed to Vox Galactica that all three are high-ranking pirate leaders convicted of complex, repeated large-scale criminal activities.
“The individuals in question were recently incarcerated at the EVE-597 reformatory because it was the only ship capable of holding them. It’s clear that the prisoner uprising was a smokescreen for their planned extraction.”
Wallglass Investigations have also released the names of the missing prisoners: Gunther Bernhardt, Yaz Murano, and Trey Coombes. They have issued further information to system authorities and warned pilots not to approach if located.
“All three should be considered armed and exceptionally dangerous,” said detective Erik Gunnarson. “Any information on their current whereabouts should be immediately reported to your nearest security contact.”
