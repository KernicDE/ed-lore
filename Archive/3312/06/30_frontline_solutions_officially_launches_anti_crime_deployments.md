Frontline Solutions has announced that its expanded deployment service has started shipping mercenaries to anti-crime operations across the core systems.
Following the successful manufacturing and test trials of the Runner transport, CEO Imogen le Ray confirmed in a press release that her corporation is ready to deploy strike teams anywhere they are needed, when they are needed, to disrupt organised crime.
“Authorities should no longer have to tackle criminal activities and coordinated piracy with stretched resources, as highlighted in recent events in the Lhou Mans system. With this new service we can augment system security to take on almost any combat or rescue situation.”
Pilots and mercenary teams willing to sign up to an operation should first travel and dock at any market port before being deployed to an available Runner.
