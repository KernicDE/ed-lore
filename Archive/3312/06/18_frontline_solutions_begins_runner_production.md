Frontline Solutions has announced the successful supply of critical components needed for the completion of its initial fleet of Runner vessels.
The new craft is capable of rapidly transporting and deploying mercenary strike teams and their ships to operational locations and engage in hostile situations as they arise.
A Frontline Solutions senior technical designer overseeing the production checks stated:
“We are extremely grateful for the support from independent pilots in supplying us with the necessary components to finalise production.”
“With the initial Runner fleet now leaving our shipyard for duty trials we’re confident that we will meet the demand and be ready to rapidly deploy combat and rescue squads wherever they’re needed.”
Frontline Solutions are set to make a formal announcement regarding the availability of its expanded service soon.
