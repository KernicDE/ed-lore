The festival marking the tenth anniversary of the founding of Colonia has begun in earnest.
Colonists throughout the Colonia enclave are welcoming visitors with open arms, showcasing their achievements over the last decade in partnership with major corporations keen to invest.
Meanwhile, major starports are hosting events and parties to attract tourists and promote the region as a place for families to relocate and build a new life outside the core systems.
“If it weren’t for the help of these kind souls, I doubt our anniversary celebrations would be as big as they are going to be,” stated Jaques in a livestreamed interview. “And my bar stands ready to welcome anyone who has worked up a thirst!”
Magen Wolf, speaking on behalf of the Colonia Council, added “The number of commodities received has provided a boost to the markets across the region.”
Local tourism chiefs have confirmed to Vox Galactica that new tourist beacons will be placed around the region, marking significant events that have occurred during Colonia’s first decade.
“We look forward to more tourism and trade! It was felt that we needed to tell our story to all who come here. And maybe some will stay to experience our next ten years,” a tourism spokesperson at Bisley Landing in Benzaiten commented.
Vox Galactica will continue to report on the celebrations as and when we are updated.
