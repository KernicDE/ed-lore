The Pilots’ Federation has issued a reminder to all members that Colonia is beginning final preparations for its 10th anniversary celebrations.
With Colonia's loaded megaship fleet due to arrive within the next few days, the Pilots’ Federation has issued a statement encouraging a final push to support the distribution of cargo at the distant enclave.
The Pilots’ Federation’s diplomatic office issued this statement:
“Any available pilots willing to deliver a wide range of commodities and rare goods for this distant community should make their way to Colonia and Jaques Station. It's important that these celebrations are an event to remember, and our members can play a crucial part in that success.”
All pilots looking to take part in the celebrations should head to Colonia and await further instructions issued from the Colonia Council.
