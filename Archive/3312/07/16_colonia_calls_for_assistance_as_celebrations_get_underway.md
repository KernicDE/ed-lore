Colonia is hosting a tenth anniversary celebration of its founding, beginning with a supply initiative located at where its historic story began.
A fleet of cargo megaships provided by Brewer Corporation has arrived at Facece to support a massive logistics operation to transport commodities and rare goods from across the core system to Colonia.
“Facece is the system where my story began,” remarked Jaques in an interview with Vox Galactica. “What better way to start the celebrations than to launch them from the very same system!”
Colonia Council member Magen Wolf lauded the campaign, adding:
“This huge effort will allow us to create a showcase for Colonia and its history and boost the economies of Colonia and surrounding systems with the potential to establish a megamarket.”
Councillor Giselle Kingspear noted that “it’s worth mentioning that we have eight Engineers established throughout the Colonia Region ready to provide services for independent pilots. We’re hoping to see a lot more business heading their way soon.”
All interested pilots should register at Peters Base at Facece before supporting the delivery of commodities and rare goods.
