The initiative to support Colonia’s tenth anniversary celebrations has been met with partial success according to organisers.
The large fleet of cargo megaships provided by Brewer Corporation stationed in Facece have been loaded and their long trip to Colonia has begun, transporting the commodities and rare goods supplied.
“The response from pilots across the core systems to our call for assistance has been appreciated,” said Magen Wolfe, speaking on behalf of Colonia Council.
“We’re still on track to make this anniversary celebration a success for the region.”
Meanwhile Jaques, via a holovid message to Vox Galactica, joked “Well, well. With the smaller number of rare goods about to hit the market it looks like my Quinentian Still will remain the favourite rare commodity in Colonia!”
With the megaship fleet now on their way, the Council is expected to issue a further call for assistance from available pilots to aid with the distribution of commodities and rare goods across regional markets within the next week.
