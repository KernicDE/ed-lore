Lady Zi’s continued belligerence in Sorbago and Exphiay is inspiring sympathetic action far from the fighting, claims Vox Galactica.
Gwendolyn Nash writes that small, nimble vessels bearing the mark of the Fire Lotus Coalition have been sighted in systems such as Facece, Nanomam and Lave:
“Dozens of starport networks, and at least three planetary government networks, have reported malicious security breaches. The twin axe motif of Lady Zi locked screens while malware ran wild through databanks and interlinked systems.”
“One source, requesting anonymity, claimed the malware behaved in a manner associated with a notorious criminal coder. Yaz Murano, one of the three fugitives of last month’s prison ship break, was serving a life sentence for trading highly classified Imperial military data obtained via network hacking. Still at large, Murano is believed to be under the personal protection of Lady Zi.”
“The fact that Sirius Corporation has ordered a full internal assessment of its current security, top-to-bottom, suggests the ability and threat of the Fire Lotus Coalition’s abilities is being taken seriously at the highest echelons of megacorporate leadership.”
