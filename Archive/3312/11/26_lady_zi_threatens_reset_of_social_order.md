The criminal figurehead Lady Zi has released a new statement via hacked newsfeeds in Shinrarta Dezhra.
Having recently instigated conflicts in the Sorbago and Exphiay systems, the Fire Lotus Coalition has now turned its attention to the home of the Pilots’ Federation. But, as Vox Galactica reports, the latest incident brings a message rather than more violence.
Lady Zi announced the following on display screens across Jameson Memorial:
“My Coalition’s recent actions invited a simple question: What is the price we pay to sustain your ivory towers?”
“I invited anybody wanting to disrupt the hegemony of our rulers to bring chaos. Many answered, with lives sacrificed to make our point. I’m sure the pampered in Sorbago and Exphiay believe our irritating protests have been quelled.”
“Military force is merely one tool of oppression. Laws and wealth are others. My personal favourite? Information.”
“The Fire Lotus Coalition has gained access to records hidden deep within superpower networks... and even those beneath the Pilots’ Federation hallowed grounds. While exploring such valued records I've corrected some notable errors.” 
“Now, I ask this: What use are laws if your lawbreakers are invisible?”
Vox Galactica has contacted all three superpowers and the Pilots’ Federation for comment.
