Pirate forces in the Sorbago and Exphiay systems have withdrawn suddenly, according to war correspondents for the Federal Times and Imperial Herald.
No warning was given prior to the retreat.
The Fire Lotus Coalition assault on Sorbago Mastopolos Mining Inc and Gallant Investment Brokers started on November 5th, amid claims from Lady Zi that the ‘obscenely wealthy’ owners were targeted for a purpose.
At the time of the withdrawal, analysts agree that the corporate defence forces had gained an advantage over the aggressors. It is thought that the Fire Lotus Coalition was being pushed back by a dogged local resistance, backed by Pilots’ Federation ships.
The Coldwood and Whisper of the Wake megaships have jumped out of their respective staging positions, withdrawing at Lady Zi’s orders. They are now docked at Swoilz DB-X b5-1.
Mastopolos Mining CEO Gabriella Mastopolos was quick to claim credit for the Fire Lotus Coalition’s withdrawal:
“I expect this self-important thief has learned her lesson: criminals testing the patience of the Empire will find themselves lacking in every aspect.”
Participants of the conflict can collect rewards from the host port where they signed up.
