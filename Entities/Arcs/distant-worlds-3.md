# Distant Worlds 3

The third installment of the legendary Distant Worlds expeditions, bringing thousands of CMDRs to the far reaches of the galaxy.

## Summary

Distant Worlds 3 (DW3) was a months-long player-organized expedition that saw over 1,000 Commanders journey from the Bubble to Beagle Point and beyond. Led by CMDR Erimus Kamzel, the expedition featured waypoints, community events, and a rich narrative chronicled on the Frontier Forums.

## Phases

1. Assembly at Pallaeni
2. The Colonia Crossing
3. The Core Transit
4. The Final Push to Beagle Point
5. Return Journey
