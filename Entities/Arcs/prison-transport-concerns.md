In mid-September 3312, several independent systems reported unusually high traffic from known pirate-affiliated pilots near prison reformatories during scheduled inmate transfers. No engagement occurred, and the vessels kept a minimum distance, but the Independent Detention Foundation flagged the pattern as unusual given many of the pilots' own criminal records. The Foundation began working with local authorities to bolster security along its transit routes.

Days later, the Foundation confirmed that the Riker-class Reformatory EVE-597 had failed to arrive at Gliese 506.2 after jumping from Nones, one of four confirmed waypoints on its prisoner transit contract alongside Evenses and Chematja. No debris was detected, and the vessel has not been sighted in any of the four systems since. The Foundation described the disappearance as concerning but stopped short of linking it to the pirate activity reported days earlier, citing EVE-597's clean service record.

On 3312-09-22, EVE-597 was discovered drifting and damaged in the Redonesses system, firmly controlled by the lawless Clan of Redonesses. Pilots noted disabled defence turrets and significant drifting debris, and the Foundation acknowledged that a "disturbance" had taken place on board during the transit window while declining to give further details. The discovery points to an ambush rather than a mis-jump, but who attacked the ship and how it reached clan territory remain unknown.
