In mid-September 3312, several independent systems reported unusually high traffic from known pirate-affiliated pilots near prison reformatories during scheduled inmate transfers. No engagement occurred, but the Independent Detention Foundation flagged the pattern as unusual given many of the pilots' own criminal records and began working with local authorities to bolster transit security. Days later, the Foundation confirmed that the Riker-class Reformatory EVE-597 had failed to arrive at Gliese 506.2 after jumping from Nones, one of four confirmed waypoints on its prisoner transit contract alongside Evenses and Chematja. No debris was detected, and the Foundation described the disappearance as concerning but stopped short of linking it to the pirate activity, citing EVE-597's clean service record.

On 3312-09-22, EVE-597 was discovered drifting and damaged in the Redonesses system, firmly controlled by the lawless Clan of Redonesses. Pilots noted disabled defence turrets and significant drifting debris, and the Foundation acknowledged that a "disturbance" had taken place on board during the transit window while declining to give further details. On 3312-10-01, a mass breakout attempt saw a large number of prisoners flee via escape pods while the Clan of Redonesses worked to shield them from recapture; the Foundation concluded the prisoners controlling the ship had chosen Redonesses precisely because its rulers were sympathetic to their cause. Independent pilots registered at the Sword of Iustitia to subdue the criminal pilots interfering with recovery.

By 3312-10-08 the breakout was subdued: EVE-597 was back under Foundation control, nearly all escapees were recaptured, and the Wallglass Investigations Agency was appointed to investigate. Detective Erik Gunnarson stated that partial telemetry indicated the breakout was coordinated with support from inside the ship, and that a pre-incident disclosure of the megaship's security data to a third party was an active line of inquiry. On 3312-10-13, the Foundation confirmed that three maximum security prisoners had evaded recapture: Gunther Bernhardt, Yaz Murano, and Trey Coombes, all high-ranking pirate leaders held at EVE-597 because it was the only ship capable of holding them. The Foundation concluded the uprising was a smokescreen for their planned extraction, while system authorities failed to elicit information from the Clan of Redonesses. Wallglass released the fugitives' names and warned pilots not to approach them.
