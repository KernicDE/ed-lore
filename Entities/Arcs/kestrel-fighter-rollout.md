Core Dynamics began rolling out its next-generation small combat fighter in February 3312 with a limited early release of the Kestrel at Polyakov Station in the Duamta system. Designed as a pure combat platform with Size 5 thrusters, supercharged Frame Shift Drive capability, and three Mk II large hardpoints, the Kestrel was explicitly positioned as the successor to the legendary Eagle Mk II.

Field trials concluded successfully in March 3312, with combat data showing the Kestrel outperforming rival small ships. CEO Owen McKenna hailed the design as the best small combat ship available and predicted adoption by both the Federal Navy and independent pilots.

On 3312-06-25, Core Dynamics announced that the Kestrel had received full distribution approval. Shipments began arriving at markets throughout the core systems, and the fighter transitioned from exclusive early release to standard shipyard availability.
