## Biography

One of four fleet carriers assigned to Distant Worlds 3. The Odysseus served as Elaine Howard's base of operations — her "Whisky Wasp" launching from its hangars on search and reconnaissance missions.

The carrier's name proved apt. Like its ancient namesake, the Odysseus wandered far from familiar shores, encountered wonders and terrors, and carried a crew who understood that the journey itself was the point. Its holo-walls displayed images of the derelict prototype. Its briefing rooms hosted route planners who traced lines across star maps with "the fussy tenderness of surgeons." Its corridors smelled of adhesive and fresh wiring — the smell of a vessel still earning its grime.

The Odysseus returned. Not all who sailed with it were as fortunate.
