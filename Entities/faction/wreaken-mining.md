## Biography

Wreaken Mining, also referred to as Wreaken Corporation, is a mining concern active in the 34th century, possibly linked to the heavy-construction contractor Wreaken Construction. In August 3312 the company's chief geologist, Dr. Ben Curtis, published research arguing that conventional laser and refinery processes waste valuable minerals because of their large-scale inefficiencies. Curtis proposed introducing a modulating waveform and tighter beam focus into a more compact laser housing, allowing planetary surfaces to be mined for resources that were previously unobtainable.

Wreaken Mining was already manufacturing deployable laser drills, refinery modules and modified Detailed Surface Scanners based on the research when, in September 3312, a joint venture with Vodel equipped the new Rhino SRV with deployable mining rigs as standard. Days after the Rhino's unveiling, the company called for independent pilots to field-test the rig's patented mining laser technology in the Ega system, registering participants at Metz Enterprise.
