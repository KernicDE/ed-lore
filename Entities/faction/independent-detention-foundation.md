## Biography

The Independent Detention Foundation operates a fleet of prison reformatory ships, including the Riker-class Reformatory EVE-597, transporting inmates between independent systems under contract. In September 3312, the Foundation reported unusually high traffic from known pirate-affiliated pilots loitering near its reformatories during inmate transfers, without direct engagement.

Days later, the Foundation confirmed that EVE-597 had failed to arrive at Gliese 506.2 after jumping from Nones, one of four confirmed waypoints on its transit contract alongside Evenses and Chematja. On 3312-09-22 the vessel was found drifting and damaged in the Redonesses system, controlled by the lawless Clan of Redonesses, and the Foundation acknowledged a "disturbance" on board during the transit window.

In October 3312, a mass prisoner breakout aboard EVE-597 was subdued with the help of independent pilots operating from the Sword of Iustitia, returning the ship to Foundation control with nearly all escapees recaptured. The Foundation appointed the Wallglass Investigations Agency to determine how the breakout was coordinated from inside the ship.

On 3312-10-13, the Foundation confirmed that three maximum security prisoners — high-ranking pirate leaders Gunther Bernhardt, Yaz Murano, and Trey Coombes — had evaded recapture, concluding that the uprising was a smokescreen for their planned extraction. Their whereabouts remain unclear, and attempts to elicit information from the Clan of Redonesses have been unsuccessful.
