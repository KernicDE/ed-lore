## Biography

Faulcon DeLacy is one of the galaxy's most respected spacecraft manufacturers, known for producing iconic ships including the Python, Cobra and Anaconda. In May 3310, the company collaborated with Achilles Aerospace to release the Python Mk II, the first ship built entirely around Achilles' Supercruise Overcharge Frame Shift Drive, featuring improved heat management, handling and fuel consumption.

The collaboration marked a significant shift in Faulcon DeLacy's traditional relationship with Sirius Corporation, which had historically dominated Frame Shift Drive manufacturing. The Python Mk II was marketed as "Built for Supercruise Overcharge," breaking speed records and establishing a new era of ship design. In June 3312, the company released the Nomad, a ship-launched exploration craft capable of independent flight and equipped with an enhanced Mk II Biological Scanner, developed in partnership with Vista Genomics for a Sanguineous Rim survey. In July 3312 the initiative was deemed a success, confirming that the Nomad and its scanner and sensor modules made exobiological research more efficient. Faulcon DeLacy's willingness to partner with a competitor to Sirius demonstrated the increasingly competitive nature of the shipbuilding industry as new technologies disrupted established supply chains. In October 3312 the company announced the Anaconda Mk II, the first major revision of the Anaconda since 2856, with an SCO-optimised FSD and Mk II component slots, launching four-way field trials at HD 232819, AD Leonis, Reorte and HR 8061 to validate it as an all-round successor.
