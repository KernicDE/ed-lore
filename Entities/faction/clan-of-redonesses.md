## Biography

The Clan of Redonesses is a lawless criminal faction that firmly controls the Redonesses system. In September 3312, the missing Independent Detention Foundation prison ship EVE-597 was discovered drifting and damaged within the Clan's territory, raising questions about possible involvement between the clan and the vessel's arrival. In October 3312, the Clan actively helped escaped EVE-597 prisoners avoid recapture, and it was believed the prisoners controlling the ship had chosen Redonesses specifically because its controlling faction was sympathetic to their cause. When three maximum security prisoners remained missing after the breakout was subdued, system authority attempts to elicit information from the Clan about the fugitives' whereabouts were unsuccessful.
