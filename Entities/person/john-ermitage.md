## Biography

Few figures in the modern exploration community carry the weight of history that John Ermitage does. An elderly strategist with tinted glasses and a calm, almost severe bearing, he was brought out of semi-stasis in 3311 to direct the Distant Worlds 3 expedition toward Orion — a route chosen not merely for its scientific promise but to monitor the volatile region around Collinder 70. Ermitage's past is entangled with the final days of GalCop; he claims to have died once and lived under an assumed identity to escape the attention of "reporters and lawyers and hitmen." He distrusts modern COVAS systems, preferring the physical levers and buttons of his vintage Lanner-class vessel, and his network of contacts extends from the Turners on Alioth to figures in the Pilots Federation who still remember the pre-Federation era. Beneath the public face of DW3 as a civilian exploration endeavour, Ermitage orchestrated a concealed agenda: the recovery and repurposing of abandoned GalCop deep space monitoring arrays from the Equinox Project at Sarasvati, the investigation of witch-space harmonic anomalies, and the placement of trusted personnel — including the enigmatic Lya — at critical points in the expedition chain of command. After the expedition he met with Cal in Tianve to discuss the Caretaker Protocol, an apparent remnant of GalCop-era AI research linked to hyperspace bleeding and matter manipulation.
