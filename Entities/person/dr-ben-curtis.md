## Biography

Dr. Ben Curtis serves as Chief Geologist for Wreaken Mining. In August 3312 he published an essay arguing that existing laser and refinery methods are so inefficient that they destroy valuable mineral resources. His research proposed using a modulating waveform and a tighter beam focus inside a more compact laser housing to mine planetary surfaces for materials that conventional techniques could not recover. Curtis's comments to the press helped frame the work as the basis for a potential new surface-mining boom.

In September 3312, Curtis announced that Wreaken would field-test the deployable mining rig built on his research, calling for independent pilots to extract materials from a variety of planetary body types and to laser-mine asteroids for comparative yield analysis, with registrations at Metz Enterprise in the Ega system. He later declared the trials a success, saying they proved his research and yielded an unprecedented amount of minerals.
