## Biography

The Imperial pilot Alessia Verdi carries the marks of hard service: lean frame, grey hair, a small Imperial crest tattooed inside her hairline. She was present at Salomé's death and came away disillusioned with hero-worship, refusing to follow saviours or lie for any power. During the Distant Worlds 3 expedition, Verdi's cynicism proved unexpectedly valuable. When the three superpowers moved to dismantle Rainbow's End and criminalise the DW3 commanders, she produced "Salomé's Requiem" — several terabytes of blackmail material drawn from all three governments — and distributed it publicly, forcing a stand-down and securing pardons for the expedition crew. Her philosophy was simple: nobody owns the truth, and nobody gets to bury it.
