## Biography

Yaz Murano is a high-ranking pirate leader convicted of complex, repeated large-scale criminal activity. Held at the EVE-597 reformatory — the only ship capable of containing them — Murano was among the maximum security inmates freed during the mass breakout orchestrated in the Redonesses system in October 3312.

The Independent Detention Foundation concluded that the prisoner uprising was a smokescreen for the planned extraction of Murano and two fellow pirate leaders, Gunther Bernhardt and Trey Coombes. Wallglass Investigations released their name and warned pilots not to approach them, describing them as armed and exceptionally dangerous; their whereabouts remain unknown.
