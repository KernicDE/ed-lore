## Biography

Magen Wolfe is a member of the Colonia Council who has publicly represented the regional government during the tenth-anniversary celebrations. In July 3312 she praised the supply initiative's potential to create a megamarket and boost the Colonia economy, and later confirmed that the effort had achieved partial success despite a smaller-than-hoped-for haul of rare goods. When the anniversary festival began in August 3312, she noted that the commodities received had provided a boost to markets across the region. When the celebrations concluded, she spoke again on behalf of the Council, thanking pilots who had travelled to Colonia and confirming that Jaques Station had become a megamarket and that six new tourist beacons had been placed around the region.
