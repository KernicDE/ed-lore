## Biography

Lady Zi is a rogue pirate admiral and the suspected leader of a criminal syndicate that conducted coordinated attacks on military installations and starport facilities in the core systems during 3312. Her true identity remains unknown, with underworld sources unwilling to divulge information about her origins or motives.

On 2 November 3312 she broke her silence with a statement delivered as malware across hundreds of hacked starport holoscreens, condemning the Federation, Empire and Alliance as oppressors and dismissing Archon Delaine's Kumo Crew as "organised thuggery, lacking strategy or long-term goals." She rallied those who fiercely oppose authority to join her growing Fire Lotus Coalition, a coalition of individuals, organisations and factions operating beyond the Kumo Crew structure. On 5 November 3312 she ordered fleet assaults on the home systems of Gallant Investment Brokers in Exphiay and Sorbago Mastopolos Mining Inc in Sorbago, with coalition megaships The Coldwood and Whisper of the Wake recruiting independent pilots to her cause.
