## Biography

Trey Coombes is a high-ranking pirate leader convicted of complex, repeated large-scale criminal activity. Held at the EVE-597 reformatory — the only ship capable of containing him — Coombes was among the maximum security inmates freed during the mass breakout orchestrated in the Redonesses system in October 3312.

The Independent Detention Foundation concluded that the prisoner uprising was a smokescreen for the planned extraction of Coombes and two fellow pirate leaders, Gunther Bernhardt and Yaz Murano. Wallglass Investigations released his name and warned pilots not to approach him, describing him as armed and exceptionally dangerous; his whereabouts remain unknown.
