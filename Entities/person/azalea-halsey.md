## Biography

Azalea Halsey has spent most of her adult life managing the fallout from her sister's visions. When Jasmina collapsed on Mars in late 3311, it was Azalea who arranged the GalNet leak, the medical indemnity protocols, and the covert diplomatic passage to Tionisla. She sat through Dr Reuben Hale's lectures on witch-space physics with a sceptic's patience, translated between Jasmina's visionary language and Felicia Winters' political necessities, and never once asked her sister to stop listening. Her practicality is the counterweight to Jasmina's leaps of faith — the ground crew for a mind that insists on flying into storms.
