## Biography

Lynda Amanda ter Holt — known to everyone as **Lya** — is an astrophysicist who hates space travel yet maps the void better than anyone. Recruited by Jasmina Halsey from ALTAS for Distant Worlds 3, she brought competitive accretion modelling and temporal theory expertise to the expedition. She established the Orion observation post, back-traced the derelict DSSA beacon to permit-locked systems, and constructed the search grid that guided the rescue detachment. When theory and practice collided, Lya chose theory. "Space is big," Halsey said. "We need to narrow our focus." Lya narrowed it. From millions of cubic light-years to a single drifting hull.
