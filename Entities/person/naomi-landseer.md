## Biography

Naomi Landseer is the chairperson of Lakon Spaceways, a major Alliance ship manufacturer known for its utilitarian and cargo-focused vessel designs. She assumed leadership during a pivotal period for the company, overseeing the development of the Type-8 Transporter — a medium-class freighter designed to accommodate the stresses of the new SCO frame shift drive. Landseer revealed that the Type-8 was moved back to the theoretical stage to properly integrate SCO technology, resulting in what became the largest medium-class freighter frame in production. Her tenure at Lakon exemplifies the Alliance industrial sector's focus on practical engineering over prestige, prioritising cargo capacity and operational efficiency. In 3312, she publicly praised Colonia as "a beacon of human achievement in deep space" while pledging Lakon's support for the settlement's tenth-anniversary celebrations.
