## Biography

The Sword of Iustitia is a megaship serving as the operational hub for the Independent Detention Foundation's enforcement campaign during the EVE-597 prison breakout in October 3312. Pilots willing to engage in combat duties against criminal pilots aiding escapees were asked to register at the vessel before handing in bounty bonds.
