## Biography

Jump satellites are a Brewer Corporation technology announced in October 3312. They modify existing beacon amplification to mimic the planetary body signal used by fleet carrier jump computers, enabling multiple fleet carriers to target and traverse systems that contain no planets or moons — a significant quality-of-life change for explorers in low star-density regions. Following a successful pilot supply initiative, the first satellites entered production calibration and were slated for deployment to HD 105341 near Rackham's Peak.
