## Biography

The Anaconda Mk II is a large multi-purpose ship announced by Faulcon DeLacy on 3312-10-20, the first major revision of the Anaconda design since its introduction in 2856. It ships with an SCO-optimised Frame Shift Drive, a Size 6 optional internal slot and Mk II armour slots with an optimised hardpoint able to mount the new mining volley repeater. Chief of Design Frank Endis positioned it as a credible trading, mining, combat and exploration vessel in equal measure, and it entered service as an early-release purchase before wider shipyard availability, with four-way field trials launched at HD 232819, AD Leonis, Reorte and HR 8061 to validate its all-round performance.
