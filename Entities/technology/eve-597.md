## Biography

EVE-597 is a Riker-class Reformatory operated by the Independent Detention Foundation, in continuous service for several years under a prisoner transit contract covering the systems Nones, Gliese 506.2, Evenses and Chematja. In September 3312 the vessel failed to arrive at Gliese 506.2 after jumping from Nones, vanishing without debris. Days later it was discovered drifting and damaged in the Redonesses system — defence turrets disabled, debris scattered — with evidence pointing to an ambush and the Foundation acknowledging a "disturbance" on board during the transit window.

In October 3312, a mass prisoner breakout saw many inmates flee via escape pods while the Clan of Redonesses shielded them from recapture. Independent pilots registered at the Sword of Iustitia helped subdue the escape effort, returning EVE-597 to Foundation control with nearly all convicts recaptured. The Wallglass Investigations Agency found that the breakout was coordinated with support from inside the ship and is examining whether its security data was leaked to a third party beforehand. The vessel is expected to resume its normal jump itinerary.
