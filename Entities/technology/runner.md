The Runner is a dedicated squad-deployment vessel developed by Frontline Solutions. It is designed to carry mercenary strike teams together with their ships to hostile or remote locations, allowing rapid response to emerging criminal or rescue scenarios. Frontline Solutions chose to build the Runner fleet independently, maintaining full control over its logistics and manufacturing chain. The first vessels completed production in mid-June 3312 and entered duty trials shortly thereafter. On 3312-06-30, Frontline officially launched anti-crime deployments using the Runner fleet, shipping mercenaries to operations across the core systems.
