## Biography

The Rhino is a heavy Surface Recon Vehicle unveiled by Vodel on 3312-09-02 as the largest SRV available. Heavily armoured, it sacrifices speed for superior grip to traverse harsh planetary terrain, and its size means it can only be deployed from a Large Vehicle Bay fitted in compatible ships.

The Rhino is the product of a joint venture between Vodel and Wreaken Mining: deployable mining rigs for planetside extraction come as standard, with an optional refinery module to process materials on site. It entered early-release purchase with wider shipyard availability planned, and Wreaken began field-testing its rig with pilots in the Ega system days after the unveiling. The trials concluded in mid-September with both companies declaring the vehicle a resounding success, citing strong terrain performance and an unprecedented mineral yield.
