## Biography

The Nomad is a ship-launched exploration vessel developed by Faulcon DeLacy and released in a limited early launch in June 3312. Unlike conventional ship-launched fighters, the Nomad can operate independently of its mothership, allowing pilots to survey tight terrain and planetary surfaces without risking their primary vessel. It is equipped with an enhanced Mk II Biological Scanner and an Mk II Sensor for detecting biological surface signals at close range. The design was marketed specifically at explorers operating larger ships, and the craft was field-tested in partnership with Vista Genomics during an exploration initiative in the Sanguineous Rim. In July 3312 the initiative was deemed a success, with pilots confirming that the Nomad and its scanner and sensor modules made exobiology sampling and research more efficient than previous explorer setups.
