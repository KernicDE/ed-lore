## Biography

HD 232819 is a system serving as the registration and staging point for the Vista Genomics and Faulcon DeLacy exploration initiative in the Sanguineous Rim. Pilots participating in the survey are directed to register at The Magrathea research megaship in this system before heading out to collect biological samples from the rim's fringe systems. The initiative was later deemed a success, validating the Nomad and its Mk II Biological Scanner and Mk II Sensor for frontier exobiology.
