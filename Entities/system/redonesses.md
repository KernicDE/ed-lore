## Biography

Redonesses is an independent system firmly controlled by the lawless Clan of Redonesses. In September 3312, the damaged and adrift prison ship EVE-597 was discovered in the system by pilots, prompting the Independent Detention Foundation to ask onlookers to keep a safe distance pending a full investigation. In October 3312, the ship's prisoners escaped en masse via escape pods while the Clan shielded them from recapture, until independent pilots helped subdue the escape effort and nearly all convicts were returned to custody.
