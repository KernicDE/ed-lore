The call went out across the galaxy, and thousands of Commanders answered. Distant Worlds 3 was not just an expedition — it was a pilgrimage to the edge of known space.

For months, the planning had been underway. Waypoints were charted, supply routes mapped, and rally points established. The destination: Beagle Point, 65,000 light-years from Sol, the furthest reachable star from the galactic centre.

On the 13th of January 3305, the fleet assembled at Pallaeni. Explorers, miners, fighters, and scientists — pilots from every background and allegiance — gathered for what would become one of the most ambitious undertakings in human history. Over a thousand registered vessels, from nimble Diamondback Explorers to massive Fleet Carriers, prepared for the months-long journey ahead.

CMDR Erimus Kamzel, the expedition's architect, addressed the fleet from the bridge of his Anaconda:

"We stand at the threshold of the unknown. What lies ahead is not just distance — it is discovery. Every jump takes us further from the familiar, closer to the frontier of human experience. Fly safe, Commanders. And remember: the black may be silent, but it is never empty."

The fleet undocked as one, a shimmering wave of navigation lights against the dark. Their course was set for Colonia, the first waypoint on a journey that would test the limits of ship, pilot, and spirit alike.
