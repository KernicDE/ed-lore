15 G. Caeli, 3311 AD

She bowed her head slightly: "Consul…"

And he waved her off. "Stop it. That was a long time ago, thank you."

"I remember," was her answer.

"Yes, we all remember, don't we? After all, that's what she told us to do." He sighed, more to himself than to her. "But a lot has or hasn't happened in the meantime. Worlds, moons and people moved on, me included."

"We will always remain who we are."

"For me I can say yes, although my father would probably disagree. But what about you? I think no one quite knows who you are."

"I was brought in to be exactly that, though. You will remember a number of assignments, because the Consulship required them of me. So like you, I am what I am — or who I was made, to be more specific."

When he said nothing she added: "Do you want to continue this philosophical debate or why did you invite me to meet your Bartender Donnie?"

"Sorry, Mir. I was just perplexed to see you packing cargo for Erimus' carrier. That's all. It's been what? Six years?"

"I am sure that is not all. But yes, almost seven."

"What are you doing here then? We could have Wilbur shipped here to take care of the fuel tanks. I'm sure he would get along well with this Timotheus guy. "

She hesitated slightly. "There are still loose ends. Connecting or severing them has been one of my tasks ever since; and even before that."

"And you are Nadya now?"

"Carlotta Inalez …vanished."

"Who?"

"An eager freelance woman in her Python during the Defence Council of Humanity days, helping evacuate refugees three years ago when the Thargoid titans attacked."

"What happened to her?"

"Her ID had to be adjusted. She would have left loose ends."

He slowly nodded. Mir was known to have a thousand aliases. It had been hard to keep track, so they let her manufacture her numerous IDs herself. The downside was, no one knew which of them was active or dormant at any given time. No one, except Mir of course…

Then it dawned on him: "Ah, the thankful refugees looking for their savior Carlotta when it was all over? That was the loose end?"

"Yes."

"And why are you working as a technician now on a carrier that will travel to the far reaches of space?"

"The Founder asked me to," she stated matter-of-factly.

"The Founder?" Andrew asked with no small amount of amazement in his voice.

"Boy, I haven't heard that name in a while. Last I remember him being named was when we chased Kajina's trinkets that she hid across K-space as contingencies."

"You remember correctly. He was there and made sure certain dots were connected, some of them probably as old as himself."

"And now he's doing it again?"

"He is making calls, yes. Calling in favors."

"Well, most of the people who owe him should be dead by now."

"Do not underestimate him, Andrew. He had enough opportunities to install contingencies himself over the years and decades. Salomé was not the only one, nor was she the first or will be the last. In fact he advised her."

"By contingencies we survive," she added.

"Such as?"

"I give you an example: Ambassador Halsey recently recruited an astrophysicist for the expedition. One of the best."

"Halsey? Jasmina Halsey?"

"Yes. Federal Ambassador to the Alliance. She recruited her directly from ALTAS, one of the premiere astrophysical institutions. Senior Fellow at 26 years if my sources are correct — which they are."

"Wow, ALTAS. That's a name second only to the Federal Astrocartography Department. She really must have made an impression to secure a Senior Fellowship."

"The Federation would certainly agree, yes. Especially after the resignation of Karl Devene after a certain setback in their acquisition of large scale astronomical data."

There it was: The very slight hint of a smile. You didn't often see it but when she smiled that smile it almost felt genuine.

"She is also one of the first graduates of Temporal Theory, a rather new field of study examining the implications of frameshift on the spacetime continuum. Very fascinating."

"Founder, Halsey, ALTAS, you, Erimus… Seems like someone is putting the band back together."

"Yes. And there is someone else."

"And who's that?"

"I…," she hesitated. "I admit I do not know. At least not yet."

"You what?" That was a shocker! There were few things her network of contacts couldn't handle and she always had been spot on in her assessments, be it on people, places or events. This alone was a deeply yellow alert.

"You don't know? You of all people… Who is he?"

"All I can say right now is he and the Founder have an agreement, albeit grudgingly. But I assume it will hold, at least for a time. Still, none of my contacts can place him. Only that he is called 'Cal' and that he apparently is very old. And this lack of information about him alone makes him potentially dangerous. People always leave traces, no matter how minute."

"Well, since we're talking about contingencies I reckon the Founder already told you to keep tabs on that 'Cal' guy?"

"Yes."

He thought for a moment, an idea dawning in the grey of his troubled brain.

"One more thing: Do you know where Alessia is?"

She raised an eyebrow at that name. "No. No one asked to keep track of her since Carcosa. But I can find out."

"Cool. I mean, yeah, great. Please do."

"I need to inform the Consulship on this, Andrew."

"No need, I'll tell them myself when the time comes."
