Built in 3306 the Drake-class Carrier was originally christened as the Shekinah. During her shakedown trip she took part in a number of stress tests, including heavy duty radiation shielding in Wolf-Rayet type environments, magnetic field stabilization in close neutron star proximity and emergency portal generation in high mass proximity. Over the span of this maiden voyage the Shekinah made her way towards the galactic rim and the Formidine Rift.

The Shekinah then took part in a number of deep space voyages and expeditions, including

Operation Caldwell 1 („Iliad“) by the Children of Raxxla in June 3306

CMDR Drew Wagar’s revisiting of the „Zurara“ in July 3306

Eagles And Angels Expedition in October 3306

Ursa Major Stream Survey in January 3308

After the Stream Survey it was decided to drydock and retrofit the Shekinah with second generation deep space sensor arrays and advanced laboratory capacities. However, what was initially intended as a routine overhaul resulted in major adjustments to and additions of capital ship class technology. In recognition of the achievements of the Ursa Major Stream Survey the Carrier was then issued a new colour code, a new callsign and re-christened as the Fimbulthul, the name of the stellar stream of stars associated with the Omega Centauri globular cluster.

However, the next assignment proved to be more conflict heavy in nature. When the first Thargoid Titans were sighted aiming for the Bubble it was decided the Fimbulthul, along with a number of other expedition carriers, was to be deployed as mobile base for advance reconnaissance and threat analysis. Because of her extensive lab facilities the ship was later intended as a forward medical base but with the deployment of eight hospital megaships by the three Powers this was deemed unnecessary.

When the fights against the Thargoid incursions intensified in the Bubble (and it became clear a civilian Carrier would be a liability instead of a reliable asset) the Fimbulthul, along with a number of core crew, was reassigned to the loose „Children Of The Core“ endeavour in May 3309. The COTC aimed at finding a number of Earth-like worlds towards the galactic core and also make contact and liaise with the Azura Initiative near the Great Annihilator black hole.

The Fimbulthul was assigned to this survey in the Inner Orion Spur until late 3310 but was recalled when another great expedition began to take shape

Enter Distant Worlds 3.

In January 3310 the Fimbulthul was added as one of the official fleet carriers to accompany the expedition flotilla as a science carrier. The carrier was subsequently refitted with a new meta-alloy based prototype radiation shielding and a recently acquired GalCop deep space sensor array. The exact purpose of this sensor array remains a mystery to date but the Fimbulthul was dispatched as a support carrier during the 'Shoulder of Orion' colonization initiative of the Orion and Barnard's Loop area, helping the Trailblazers to scout a route along the difficult, permit-locked COL 70 area.

After further field testing and shakedown cruises and an expansive crew reshuffling the Fimbulthul joined the DW3 flotilla in the NGC 1981 cluster area as a DSEC, Deep Space Exploration Carrier, and began – along with the three other carriers – the four-month trek on January 18th from the designated launch system.

The Fimbulthul has been on assignment since.
